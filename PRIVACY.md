# Privacy Policy for Dark Mode for Google Docs

Last updated: 2026-11-17

Dark Mode for Google Docs does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no servers, analytics, advertising or third-party services, and it makes no network requests.

## What the extension stores

All of it stays on your computer:

- **Your settings.** Whether dark mode is on, and the colours you saved on the options page, are kept in the extension's own browser storage.
- **A copy of your colours on docs.google.com.** The extension keeps your colours in the browser storage of docs.google.com, so a document opens dark without a flash of white. It holds only colour values, which the extension never sends anywhere. Google's own pages can read it, as they can anything stored for docs.google.com.

## Your documents

To theme a document, the extension changes the colours Google Docs draws with, in your browser. It does not collect, keep or send the content of your documents. It runs only on Google Docs documents, at `https://docs.google.com/document/`, and not on any other site.

## Deleting your data

**Use default** on the options page removes your saved colours. Uninstalling the extension removes its settings. The copy of your colours on docs.google.com stays until you clear the site data for docs.google.com in Chrome's settings.

## Changes to this policy

If what the extension stores ever changes, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the date above will change.
